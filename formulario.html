Formulario

Átomo

  • \(\tilde\nu=R_H(1/n_f^2-1/n_i^2)\), \(R_H=109\,677\) cm⁻¹
  • \(E_n=-13{,}6/n^2\) eV; \(r_n=n^2a_0\)
  • \(\lambda=h/mv\); \(\Delta x\Delta p\ge h/4\pi\)
  • 1 eV = 1,602·10⁻¹⁹ J = 96,49 kJ/mol

Enlace

  • \(U=Q+S+\tfrac12D+I-E\) (Born-Haber)
  • \(U=-\dfrac{N_AAz_1z_2e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r_0}\left(1-\dfrac1n\right)\)
  • Carga formal \(=n_v-n_{nb}-\tfrac12n_b\)
  • \(\mu=\delta d\); 1 D = 3,34·10⁻³⁰ C·m; \(OE=\tfrac12(n_e-n_{ae})\)

Gases

  • \(PV=nRT\); \(\rho=PM/RT\); \(P=\rho gh\)
  • \(P_i=\chi_iP\); \(u_{rms}=\sqrt{3RT/M}\)
  • \(\left(P+\dfrac{an^2}{V^2}\right)(V-nb)=nRT\)
  • \(n\lambda=2d\sin\theta\); \(\gamma=rh\rho g/2\cos\theta\)

Estequiometría

  • \(n=m/M=N/N_A=PV/RT=MV\)
  • \(M_1V_1=M_2V_2\); \(M=10\rho\%/M_{soluto}\)
  • Limitante: menor n/coeficiente
  • Rendimiento = real/teórico × 100

Termodinámica

  • \(\Delta U=q+W\); \(W=-P\Delta V\); \(W_{rev}=-nRT\ln(V_2/V_1)\)
  • \(\Delta U=nC_v\Delta T\); \(\Delta H=nC_p\Delta T\); \(C_p-C_v=R\)
  • \(\Delta H=\Delta U+\Delta n_{gas}RT\)
  • \(\Delta H_r^0=\sum\nu\Delta H_f^0\); Kirchhoff \(\Delta H_{T_2}=\Delta H_{T_1}+\Delta C_p\Delta T\)

Entropía y Gibbs

  • \(\Delta S=nR\ln(V_2/V_1)=nC_p\ln(T_2/T_1)=\Delta H/T\)
  • \(\Delta S_r^0=\sum\nu S^0\)
  • \(\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S\le0\)
  • \(\Delta G=nRT\ln(P_2/P_1)\) (isotermo)

Equilibrio

  • \(K_p=K_c(RT)^{\Delta n}\); \(\Delta G^0=-RT\ln K\)
  • \(\ln\dfrac{K_2}{K_1}=\dfrac{\Delta H^0}{R}\left(\dfrac1{T_1}-\dfrac1{T_2}\right)\)
  • \(K_p=\dfrac{4\alpha^2P}{1-\alpha^2}\) (A ⇌ 2B)
  • \(T_{K=1}=\Delta H^0/\Delta S^0\)

Fases y disoluciones

  • \(L=C-F+2\); \(\dfrac{dP}{dT}=\dfrac{\Delta H}{T\Delta V}\)
  • \(\ln\dfrac{P_2}{P_1}=\dfrac{\Delta H_v}{R}\left(\dfrac1{T_1}-\dfrac1{T_2}\right)\)
  • \(P_i=\chi_iP_i^*\); \(P_B=K_B\chi_B\)
  • \(\Delta T_b=iK_bm\); \(\Delta T_f=iK_fm\); \(\pi=icRT\)

Electroquímica y pH

  • \(E=E^0-\dfrac{0{,}059}{n}\log Q\); \(\Delta G=-nFE\)
  • pH = −log[H⁺]; pH + pOH = 14
  • \([H^+]=\sqrt{K_aC}\); \(m=MIt/nF\)
